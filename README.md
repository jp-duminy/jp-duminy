## Hi, I'm Jean-Paul (JP)

I am an astrophysicist in my MPhys year at the University of Edinburgh. My current research focus is on modelling the observational signatures of Population III stars using high-resolution galaxy simulations; I am also the lead developer and maintainer of [octavius](https://octavius.readthedocs.io/en/latest/).

### Recent Projects

- [octavius](https://github.com/jp-duminy/octavius): a high-performance simulation analysis toolkit capable of tackling next-generation simulations (currently working on a publication)
- [Pop3](https://github.com/jp-duminy/pop3): constructing photoionisation models of Population III stars with the Pop2Prime simulation
- [Cepheids](https://github.com/jp-duminy/Cepheids-Telescope-Group-Project-2025-26): a bespoke reduction, photometry and analysis pipeline to derive the distance to Andromeda from Cepheid variable stars
- [Modelling and Visualisation](https://github.com/jp-duminy/Modelling-and-Visualisation-in-Physics): core simulation techniques, including Monte Carlo methods, cellular automata, and PDE solvers

### Interests
